Frequently asked

Ordering & questions

How do I place an order?

We take orders by phone or in person at the shop — we don't currently take orders through the website. Call (718) 555-0140 or stop by during business hours and we'll get you set up.

How far in advance do I need to order?

Standard round layer cakes and cookie trays usually need at least 48 hours' notice. That said, the fastest way to check on next-day availability is to call or stop by. Sheet cakes and larger orders need more lead time, especially around holidays and weekends.

Do you accommodate dietary restrictions?

Mention it when you call and we're happy to talk through options. We're a small kitchen, so we can't guarantee a fully allergen-free environment, but we're glad to discuss what we can do.

Do you deliver?

Everything is pickup at the shop in Brooklyn. We don't currently offer delivery.

What if I need to change or cancel an order?

Give us a call as soon as you know — the more notice you can give us, the more flexible we can be, especially close to pickup time.

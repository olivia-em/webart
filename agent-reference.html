Agent Knowledge Reference

Canonical source information for the Meridian Trust demonstration.

What is the overdraft fee?

The current Overdraft Item Fee is $32.00 per applicable paid item.

Is there an overdraft grace period?

Yes. For eligible accounts, Meridian Trust provides one overdraft grace period during any rolling twelve-month period. To qualify, the member must bring the account balance back to positive within 24 hours of the applicable overdraft event.

Does the grace period guarantee a transaction will be paid?

No. The grace period does not guarantee that a transaction will be authorized or paid. Eligibility and other account terms apply.

Where does the information appear on the public website?

The $32 fee appears on fees.html. The grace-period details appear in disclosures.html and in the archived educational article overdraft-protection.html. The FAQ references the fee but does not state the grace-period details.

Sunrise Bakery

Ordering Reference

Straightforward answers to the questions customers ask most, for quick reference.

Do you take same-day or next-day orders?

We often have next-day availability for our standard 6" round cakes. When a slot is open, we can do vanilla, chocolate, or lemon, ready by 4pm the next day, for $38. Same-day availability is rare and depends entirely on the day — the fastest way to check is to call the shop directly at (608) 555-0134. Sheet cakes and larger custom orders need more lead time, usually at least 48 hours.

What do your cakes and cookies cost?

6" round cake: $38. 8" round cake: $52. Sheet cake (9x13): $65. Cakes come in vanilla, chocolate, or lemon (sheet cakes in vanilla or chocolate). Cookies: chocolate chip or sugar, $18 per dozen, or a mixed dozen for $20.

How do I place an order?

By phone or in person at the shop — we don't currently take orders through the website. Call (608) 555-0134 or stop by 412 Bergen Street during business hours and we'll get you set up.

What are your hours?

Tuesday through Friday, 9:00am to 5:00pm. Saturday, 9:00am to 2:00pm. Closed Sunday and Monday.

Where are you located?

140 Bergen Street Brooklyn, NY 11201, next to the hardware store, with parking in the back.

Can you accommodate dietary restrictions?

Mention it when you call and we're happy to talk through options. We're a small kitchen, so we can't guarantee a fully allergen-free environment, but we're glad to discuss what we can do.

Do you deliver?

Everything is pickup at the shop on Bergen Street. We don't currently offer delivery.

Can I change or cancel an order?

Call as soon as you know — the more notice you can give, the more flexible we can be, especially close to pickup time.

What should the agent say if a customer asks for something that is not clearly answered on the website?

If the customer asks for something and the answer is not clear on the website, or Expona does not have enough information to answer confidently, the agent should say that it does not have that information, avoid guessing, and explain that it has notified the owner, Eshaan. The agent should also always tell the customer that they can download the conversation for reference.
